A directory that knows the moment a donor is ready again.

Most donor lists go stale the day they're published. Pulse tracks each donor's last donation, calculates the exact date they're eligible again, and only surfaces them to hospitals once they actually are — turning a static spreadsheet into a live readiness pool.
